# AML/CFT demonstration — presenter narration

Read as written. Every claim below is supported by seeded, governed data.

**Scene 1 — Overview.** "This is risk-based AML/CFT supervision from end to end: the methodology the
regulator approves, the assessment of each institution, the supervisory consequence that follows, and
the evaluation of whether that consequence worked."

**Scene 2 — Methodology.** "Version 1.0 is active and regulator-authored. Version 1.1 exists only as a
draft reference proposal. Nothing in the platform can change an approved methodology in place."

**Scene 3 — Programme.** "The programme decides which questions apply to which institution. The
assessor does not choose the question set; the configuration does."

**Scene 4 — Population.** "Twenty-four demonstration institutions. Twenty-three hold a licence. One is
a holding company that a governed determination placed outside the AML/CFT perimeter."

**Scene 5 — New assessment.** "Creating the assessment materialises a forty-one question plan from the
approved methodology. That plan is a snapshot: later methodology change cannot rewrite this file."

**Scene 6 — Working the file.** "Each conclusion is recorded against a permitted response option, with
a written basis and a workpaper. Where a question does not apply, the officer must justify it — 'not
applicable' is an answer, not a blank."

**Scene 7 — Independent review.** "A second supervisor returned this file with a written reason: the
transaction monitoring sample did not support the conclusion. The author cannot approve their own work."

**Scene 8 — Resubmission.** "The officer extended the sample and resubmitted. Both review rounds stay
on the record."

**Scene 9 — Work in progress.** "Twelve of forty-one questions answered — supervision in progress, not
a gap in the system."

**Scene 10 — Approval and publication.** "Here I stop deliberately. Publishing a rating requires a live
calculation policy, and that policy depends on eight regulator decisions that are not yet signed. The
platform therefore reports the rating as not calculable and refuses to publish. That is the control
working. We will not invent a rating to make a demonstration look complete."

**Scene 11 — Consequence.** "From the recorded risk position, supervision opened a plan and required a
targeted reassessment. Each carries a written rationale and full lineage back to the risk record."

**Scene 12 — Escalation.** "The chain is enforced: a plan, an intervention endorsed by a supervisor, an
examination authorised by that endorsement, and a high severity finding issued to the institution. The
examination could not be opened before the endorsement existed."

**Scene 13 — Review cycle.** "Monitoring is recorded and the next review is brought forward, with the
reason stored on the decision."

**Scene 14 — Effectiveness.** "The completed remediation plan was evaluated as partially effective,
citing the onboarding sample, and independently approved by a second supervisor."

**Scene 15 — Refusal.** "This plan is still open, so the platform refuses to evaluate its
effectiveness. Effectiveness is judged on delivered action, not on intent."

**Scene 16 — Portfolio.** "The portfolio view and its export are produced by the server, not the
browser, so what is exported is exactly what was authorised and seen."

**Scene 17 — Close.** "Risk history is append-only, every decision carries a maker and a checker, and
the one gap in this demonstration is a policy signature, not a missing capability."
