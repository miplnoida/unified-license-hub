# Step 1: turn on 3 Credit Union services (as Super Admin)

The preview is now signed in as super_admin@mishainfotech.com.

1. Open Admin, then Master Configuration, then the Services tab.
2. Change the status from **inactive** to **active** for these 3 existing services, using the normal edit dialog:
   - Variation of Licence Conditions (CU-PL-15)
   - Change in Authorized Activities (CU-PL-10)
   - Change of Compliance Officer / MLRO (CU-PL-07)
3. Reload the page and check that all 3 show as active.

This makes these services available to every licensed credit union: Rodney Bay and Piton Community Credit Union Ltd.

The other 12 services stay inactive. No forms, services, permissions or licences change.
